# Data and security policy

CERTAIN-COAST concerns critical infrastructure (drinking-water wellfields, a port and an energy landfall).

* This repository holds **code, documentation and synthetic data only**.
* Never commit: data provided by end-users or well owners, results for an individual asset, sensor or well locations,
  thresholds of operational alarms, or any file marked restricted.
* Before every release, the release check of the project's IPR agreement (Article 5.4) is applied, and the end-user
  reviews any publication that uses its data.
* Restricted data stay on the project's servers under the data management plan (D7.1).
